Company Overview:

PT Barito Renewables Energy Tbk. (BREN.JK), listed on the Indonesian Exchange, operates in the Electric Utilities industry under the Infrastructures sector. The company provides renewable energy solutions with a focus on electricity and steam production. It has a market capitalization rank of 2, employing around 679 individuals as of now.

Financial Performance:

BREN's financial performance has shown consistent growth over the years. The company's earnings per share (EPS) has increased from 12.4 in 2023 to 16.54 in 2025. Even the revenue has followed the same trend, increasing from IDR 7.64Trillion in 2021 to IDR 10.13Trillion in 2025. However, a dip was noticed in Q4 2024's revenue to IDR 2.51Trillion.

Revenue Breakdown:

The company generates a significant portion of its revenue from the 'Sales of Electricity' (IDR 4.5Trillion) followed by 'Sales of Steam' (IDR 1.97Trillion). Other sources include management fees, operating and finance lease income, and carbon credits.

Investment Analysis:

BREN's successful growth in EPS and revenue over the years and its dominance in the renewable energy sector presents a strong case for investment. However, caution must be exercised due to the recent dip in quarterly revenues. Potential investors may need to watch for the Q1 2025 results to determine whether the dip in Q4 2024 was just an outlier or a sign of a downtrend.
